TASK: VISUAL QA AND REFINEMENT OF THE STATIC HERO
=================================================

The static hero now exists. Do not redesign it and do not add new features. Your job is to perform a rigorous visual comparison, correct proportions and leave the static hero ready for interaction work.

First:

1. Read docs/Rishav_Web_Studio_DESIGN.md.
2. Inspect references/hero-approved.png.
3. Inspect the current hero implementation and its styles.
4. Run the app and capture fresh screenshots of the rendered site.
5. If I attached a screenshot of the current result, treat it as additional evidence of what is wrong.


TARGET VIEWPORTS
----------------
Check at least:

- 1440 × 1000
- 1280 × 800
- 390 × 844
- 430 × 932

Use side-by-side or overlay-style comparison if your available browser/image tools support it. Reason from the rendered result, not only from the CSS values.


FIX IN THIS PRIORITY ORDER
--------------------------
1. Main heading proportion
   - RISHAV must dominate without occupying the full width.
   - WEB STUDiO must be slightly smaller and much wider.
   - Leading must be tight and intentional, not cramped by accident.
   - The full composition must have the same editorial mass and dark breathing room as the reference.

2. Bulb integration
   - The lowercase i remains readable.
   - The bulb must be a full, generous pear shape, not narrow or pinched.
   - Keep a visible 8–12px apparent gap between the bulb/socket and i stem at large desktop scale.
   - Keep the socket short and separate.
   - The stem should be sturdy, not hairline-thin.
   - Keep the bulb larger than a normal dot and allow it to cross the upper guide/cap-height.
   - Preserve a warm luminous core and controlled local halo.
   - Keep the cord thin, centred and visually clean.

3. Background and contrast
   - The background should be darker than a normal navy website: a chromatic blue-black.
   - Do not brighten the entire scene to make decoration visible.
   - Increase the line colour/opacity of grid and doodles instead.
   - Grid lines should be pale blue-white and restrained.
   - Doodle lines should be somewhat more visible than the grid but still atmospheric.
   - The title remains the brightest and most dominant element.

4. Utility layout
   - Top-left wordmark and top-right navigation should feel aligned to one quiet baseline.
   - Bottom-left information and bottom-right scroll prompt should feel deliberate, not pasted into corners.
   - Utility type must be small but not unreadably tiny.

5. Mobile composition
   - Do not scale down the desktop canvas as one object.
   - Preserve the title's impact and two-line structure.
   - Simplify or reposition studio doodles.
   - Prevent collisions between the cord, title and bottom copy.
   - Avoid horizontal clipping and accidental scrollbars.


IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------
- Keep the current React/TypeScript architecture unless it is actively causing a problem.
- Use CSS and small SVG adjustments; do not rasterise the composition.
- Do not use the reference image in the shipped interface.
- Do not add GSAP, Framer Motion, physics libraries, scroll effects or pointer parallax.
- Do not begin another homepage section.
- Do not add generic decoration to fill space.
- Preserve accessibility and semantic text.


QUALITY BAR
-----------
The result should feel like an art-directed studio homepage, not a close-enough coding mockup. Correct obvious font fallback, weak line-height, arbitrary margins, poor kerning, hidden doodles, over-bright gradients and unbalanced negative space.

Use design tokens or custom properties for repeatable colours, spacing and type scales. Remove dead CSS and temporary debugging styles once the corrections are complete.


VALIDATION
----------
After editing:

1. Capture fresh screenshots at all four target sizes.
2. Check keyboard focus and semantic heading/nav behaviour.
3. Run the production build.
4. Check for console errors and horizontal overflow.

Then report:

- The visual problems you found.
- The specific corrections you made.
- The files changed.
- The screenshots/viewports checked.
- Any remaining mismatch that requires a human design decision.

Stop after the static visual QA. Do not add the interactive bulb until I approve the appearance.
