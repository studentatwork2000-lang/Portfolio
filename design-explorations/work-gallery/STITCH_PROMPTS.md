# Rishav Web Studio — Work Gallery Concepts

These prompts are intended for Google Stitch. Use the current Work-section screenshot as structural context and the over-the-center editorial carousel image as inspiration only. Do not copy the reference site's branding, typography, or exact proportions.

## Shared direction

Create a polished desktop concept for only the “Glimpses of My Work” section of a premium personal web studio portfolio. Preserve the existing black, midnight-navy, and royal-blue gradient environment. The visual language is moody, editorial, art-directed, restrained, and implementation-ready—not generic SaaS and not a stock carousel.

Show the exact text:

- “Glimpses of My Work”
- “01 / 04”
- “Restaurant”
- “Hospitality / Web Design”

Keep all active-project metadata centered above the gallery. Do not place labels or text inside the image frames.

Use one dominant active landscape frame around 48–56vw wide and 44–50svh tall, with an editorial ratio around 1.75:1–1.9:1. Previous and next frames must each retain roughly 22–30vw of readable width. Use a restrained 10–14px radius and an extremely subtle cool blue-gray hairline border. Add only a gentle blue environmental rim light; avoid heavy drop shadows.

Use custom open chevrons made from two softly rounded strokes, resembling a wider-angle editorial chevron. Visible size 14–18px with a larger invisible hit area. No shaft, circle, pill, or filled background.

The static composition should support a future “peek → expand → center” transition: the incoming neighbor grows and brightens toward center while the outgoing active card recedes and darkens. No bounce, elastic movement, or dramatic 3D rotation.

Do not use browser chrome, fake UI, three equal rectangles, skinny pillar neighbors, giant arrows, excessive labels, excessive rounding, glassmorphism, neon borders, WebGL spectacle, or Cover Flow. Do not redesign the background, Hero, Intro, or the rest of the page.

## Concept A — Layered Editorial Focus

Place the active Restaurant project in the exact visual center at roughly 52vw wide and 46svh tall. Keep both neighboring project frames clearly visible at roughly 24–27vw of readable width. Position the neighbors slightly behind the active card, with the active card overlapping their inner edges modestly. Create depth through scale, opacity, brightness, position, and z-order only. Keep the active card crisp and luminous; make the neighbors 92–94% scale and slightly darker. Keep all cards front-facing. This is the closest concept to the editorial reference while remaining original.

## Concept B — Asymmetric Cinematic Gallery

Keep the active Restaurant project dominant but offset it slightly left of center. Crop the previous card more decisively at the left viewport edge, leaving about 20–22vw readable. Give the incoming right card a more open runway and about 28–30vw readable. Let the active card overlap the incoming card's inner edge slightly while the previous card sits farther behind. Use asymmetric negative space to imply forward transition direction while preserving elegant horizontal navigation. The result should resemble an art, architecture, or editorial portfolio rather than a conventional carousel. No perspective tilt.

## Concept C — Depth-Stack Gallery

Center the active Restaurant project at roughly 50–53vw wide and visually bring it forward. Place both neighboring project frames behind it and 22–30px lower. Keep each neighbor around 90–93% scale with 23–27vw readable. The active card should overlap both inner edges modestly. Use only z-order, scale, opacity, brightness, and the small vertical drop to create depth. Keep every card front-facing with zero perspective rotation. The static frame should suggest that an incoming neighbor will brighten, grow, and rise toward center while the active card darkens, shrinks, and lowers.
